Go to Releases to get EarthOS. Built with care and love by me :)

EarthOS version naming is themed after nature (plants & flowers). It has its own kernel (started from bottom-0, not forked on any other scratchOS builds).
I have developed an intrest for scratchOS back in 2024, but never made a full working version. Back in 24 July 2025, I got the interest back, wanting to complete the OS for its public release. After 5 months, on 3 January 2026, my first version (1.17) has finally been released.
